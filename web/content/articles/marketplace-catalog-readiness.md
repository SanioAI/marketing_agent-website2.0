Feeds validate yet listings suppress, match wrong ASINs, or trigger compliance flags; teams lack a single readiness definition across channels.

## Proof Points

- **Voomi Supply:** readiness = automated **product matching**, **multipack detection**, **hazmat** at **1M+ SKUs** / **200M+ ASINs** with **~85% faster publish**—channel bots do not forgive “feed green.”
- **Profitero:** readiness across **1500+ marketplaces** requires normalized labels at **>95% precision/recall**, not per-channel copy variants.
- **JCPenney:** **Mirakl + SFCC** onboarding—readiness bottleneck is **taxonomy alignment across brands**, not JPEG quality.
- **Failure scenario:** **channel taxonomy misalignment** → wrong browse node → suppression or wrong detail page traffic.
