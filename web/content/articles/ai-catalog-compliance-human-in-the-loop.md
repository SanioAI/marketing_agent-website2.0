Agents propose; **humans approve** on low-confidence, high-liability slices—same pattern as **Profitero** shrinking manual labeling **140 → ~20** while holding **>95% precision/recall**: automation absorbs the long tail, experts absorb the tail risk. **Voomi** replaced rule+VA with automation but **hazmat** and **multipack** remain exactly the class of decisions where HITL thresholds matter at **1M+ SKU** scale.

Legal blocks “AI compliance” wholesale or green-lights unchecked bots—both fail at **JCPenney**-scale marketplace exposure.


## Proof Points

- **Profitero:** **140 → ~20** manual labeling with **>95% precision/recall**—model for constrained automation + expert review.  
- **Voomi Supply:** **hazmat** + **multipack** in automation replacing rule+VA at **1M+ SKUs** / **200M+ ASINs**, **~85%** faster publish.  
- **JCPenney:** marketplace onboarding complexity—humans on brand-supplier edge cases.  
- **Failure scenario:** zero HITL on novel battery chemistry → wrong hazmat class shipped.
