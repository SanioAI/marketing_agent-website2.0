The **Brand Normalization Agent** produces **canonical brand keys** so **Channel Matching** and marketplace browse trees do not fork on spelling—foundational for **Profitero** across **1000+ brands** (labeling **140 → ~20** with **>95% P/R**) and for **JCPenney** multi-brand **Mirakl** programs. Without it, **Voomi**-scale **200M+ ASIN** disambiguation fails on tie-breakers.

“ACME”, “Acme Co”, “ACME™” split ASIN candidates and Mirakl vendor tiles—rules lists explode.
