## Proof Points

- **Voomi Supply:** checklist items must catch what their automation replaced—**multipack detection**, **hazmat**, **ASIN matching**—otherwise **~85% publish-time gain** is not repeatable on new categories.
- **Profitero:** “done” = precision/recall gates (**>95%**) on labeling pipelines, not checkbox coverage across **1500+ marketplaces**.
- **JCPenney:** onboarding checklist must include **Mirakl/SFCC taxonomy alignment** per brand—failure = wrong browse node / variant family.
- **Failure scenario:** checklist green on “description” but **channel taxonomy misalignment** → listing rejection or wrong SERP bucket.
