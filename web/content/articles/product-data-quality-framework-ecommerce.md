Quality for agentic commerce is **decision correctness**, not field coverage: ASIN wrong-rate, multipack defect rate, hazmat false negative rate, taxonomy misalignment count per brand—metrics visible in programs like **Voomi** (**1M+ SKUs**, **~85%** faster publish after fixing those classes) and **Profitero** (**>95% precision/recall**, **140 → ~20** labeling). PIM “% complete” is a **lagging vanity metric** for AI.

DQ scorecards are green while marketplace revenue drops—execs lose trust in data org.
