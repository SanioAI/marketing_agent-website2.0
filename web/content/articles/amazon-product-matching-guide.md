ASIN matching is **entity resolution at commerce speed** with high cost of error (suppression, IP risk, wrong detail page). **Channel Matching Agent** treats matching as a scored, auditable decision with evidence—not manual search-and-click at scale.

Spreadsheet ASIN pickers do not survive **Voomi** volume: **1M+ SKUs** against **200M+** candidates needs scored, auditable matching—the same workload that helped unlock **~85% publish-time** reduction when automation replaced rule+VA loops. **Profitero**-class programs think in **>95% precision/recall**, not “most common ASIN.” **JCPenney** Mirakl mixes multiply ambiguous ties—without **brand normalize + pack count + variation lineage**, you ship detail-page risk, not “close enough.”
