Fitment is **structured constraint data**, not prose in a description. The **Compatibility Agent** and **Product Graph Agent** maintain bidirectional truth (SKU ↔ vehicle/model/application) with evidence, versioning, and conflict detection—so agents and marketplaces do not “guess” fit.

Fitment is trapped in PDFs while **Voomi** proves **1M+ SKUs** need machine-checkable application tables to stay adjacent to **200M+ ASINs** safely; **Profitero** proves multilingual constraint labels need **>95% P/R** discipline, not “universal fit” prose; **JCPenney** Mirakl exposes when **taxonomy + variant families** disagree with supplier application data—reviews and returns are symptoms of **graph debt**, not copy debt.

## Proof Points

- **Voomi Supply:** HVAC **1M+ SKUs**—fitment-grade constraints required for safe **200M+ ASIN** adjacency; **compatibility** errors drive returns before agent era, more so after.
- **Profitero:** constrained classification at **>95% precision/recall** across **80+ languages** is the discipline fitment tables need—each application row is a labeled constraint, not marketing language dressed as structure.
- **JCPenney:** multi-brand parts/accessories on marketplace—**taxonomy + fitment** must align to channel variant models.
- **Failure scenario:** “universal fit” marketing copy with **compatibility gaps** in structured application table → preventable return.
