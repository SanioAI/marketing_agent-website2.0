**TaxGraph** is Paladio’s name for the **taxability subgraph**: SKUs, packs, categories, jurisdictions, exemptions, and bundle relationships that tax engines should consume as **structured intelligence**, not one-off overrides. It composes with **Compliance Agent** outputs (e.g., **hazmat**, **multipack** from **Voomi**-style automation) and with **channel taxonomy** truth (**JCPenney** / **Mirakl**).

Finance buys a tax engine; catalog still ships **multipack** and category drift; auditors find SKU-level contradictions—no graph, no defense.
