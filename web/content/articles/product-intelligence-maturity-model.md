## Why legacy maturity models fail AI commerce

Most maturity models claim to measure success through field coverage and data governance. But here's the twist: these models miss the mark because they overlook the true indicators of maturity — **decision correctness** and **publish velocity**. Your catalog doesn't lag because you lack fields. It stumbles because your system can't autonomously decide and publish at the speed your market demands.

The real problem isn’t the absence of data governance. It's the inability to scale decision-making across millions of SKUs with precision. For instance, Voomi Supply didn't transform by merely aligning attributes; they achieved maturity by matching **200M+ ASINs** and reducing publish time by **~85%**. This kind of operational efficiency isn't captured by traditional maturity models, which stop at "governed PIM" and fail to push into the realm of agent-driven intelligence.

## Understanding the stages to maturity

The path from static records to dynamic intelligence isn't linear. It's a series of transformations that most maturity models overlook. Let's break it down into stages, each with specific exit KPIs that reflect true intelligence maturity:

1. **Static Records**: Your starting point. Here, you have data but no actionable intelligence. Exit KPI: Basic attribute collection.
   
2. **Normalized Intelligence**: Data begins to speak to each other. Attributes harmonize. Exit KPI: **10–15+ attributes** standardized as seen in Voomi's initial steps.

3. **Graph Intelligence**: Relationships between data points form. You move beyond flat files to interconnected insights. Exit KPI: Automated multipack detection like Voomi achieved.

4. **Compliance Intelligence**: Your system aligns with external requirements, such as **hazmat** classification. Exit KPI: >95% precision/recall akin to Profitero’s stage achievements.

5. **Continuous Agents**: Here, agents don't just process; they decide and execute. Exit KPI: The ability to handle **1500+ marketplaces** and **80+ languages** with the precision Profitero maintains.

Skipping stages or misjudging your current one can lead to false maturity, like missing the "multipack truth," which creates a deceptive sense of readiness. Each stage serves as a necessary foundation for the next.

## Mapping Paladio agents per stage

Paladio agents are the linchpin that connects these maturity stages, removing friction at every turn. In the **Normalized Intelligence** stage, Paladio agents ensure your attributes are **harmonized** across various platforms.
