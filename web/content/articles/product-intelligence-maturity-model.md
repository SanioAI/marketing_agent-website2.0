Maturity is measured by **decision correctness** and **publish velocity**, not field coverage: stages map to what **Voomi** had to achieve (**10–15+ attributes**, **multipack**, **hazmat**, **200M+ ASIN** matching at **1M+ SKUs**, **~85%** faster publish) and what **Profitero** enforces (**>95% precision/recall**, **1500+ marketplaces**, **140→20** labeling). **JCPenney**-stage adds **Mirakl/SFCC** taxonomy alignment as an explicit gate.

Maturity models from vendors stop at “governed PIM”; they never force **channel-bot** or **agent** KPIs.
