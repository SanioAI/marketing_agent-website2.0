PIM optimizes **governance and publication** of product records; product intelligence optimizes **correctness of machine decisions** across channels and agents. Paladio uses **catalog agents** to close the gap PIM workflows were not designed to measure: normalization, graph, matching, compliance at SKU scale.

PIM is paid for; **Amazon/Mirakl/Google** bots still reject or mis-rank SKUs. **Voomi** proved “record complete” ≠ **1M+ SKU → 200M+ ASIN** truth without **multipack/hazmat** intelligence (**~85% publish-time** came from changing KPIs, not adding PIM seats). **Profitero** shows **>95% P/R** is the intelligence KPI, not field coverage across **1500+ marketplaces**. **JCPenney** shows **Mirakl** browse alignment is the KPI, not internal workflow green lights. You need vocabulary that respects PIM budgets but names the **missing intelligence layer**.

## Proof Points

- **Voomi KPI:** “feed valid” vs **wrong ASIN / multipack** rate—**~85% faster publish** required intelligence KPIs, not PIM completeness on **1M+ SKUs**.
- **Profitero KPI:** “labels created” vs **>95% precision/recall** across **80+ languages**—intelligence is classifier quality.
- **JCPenney KPI:** count **taxonomy alignment defects per brand-supplier slice** during **Mirakl + SFCC** onboarding (wrong browse node / variant family)—track backlog burn-down alongside listing readiness; pair in prose with **Voomi**’s **~85% publish-time** and **Profitero**’s **>95% P/R** so JCP isn’t the only paragraph without adjacent hard numbers.
- **Failure scenario:** PIM-complete + **channel taxonomy misalignment** → suppressed listing despite “100% required fields.”
