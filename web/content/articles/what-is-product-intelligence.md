**Product intelligence** is machine-actionable truth about SKUs: normalized attributes, stable identities, graph relationships, compliance/taxability facts, and channel alignment—maintained under change. It is the substrate for **catalog readiness** and the fuel for **catalog agents**, not a rebranding of “more fields in the PIM.”

“Product intelligence” slides read like BI dashboards. Operations need the same concrete bundle **Voomi** runs for **1M+ SKUs** (**ASIN identity**, **multipack**, **hazmat**, **~85% publish-time** improvement), **Profitero** enforces across **1500+ marketplaces** (**>95% P/R**, **140→20** labeling), and **JCPenney** needs for **Mirakl/SFCC** (**taxonomy-aligned** identities)—machine-decidable facts, not more chart widgets.

## Proof Points

- **Profitero:** intelligence = normalized signals usable across **1500+ marketplaces** with measurable **>95% precision/recall**, not “more fields.”
- **Voomi Supply:** intelligence bundle includes **ASIN identity**, **multipack truth**, **hazmat**—how **1M+ SKUs** stayed matchable to **200M+ ASINs**.
- **JCPenney:** intelligence = **taxonomy-aligned** facet keys + brand-stable identities for marketplace onboarding—not marketing copy.
- **Failure scenario:** rich “information” record but **missing attributes** for voltage → agent cannot rank safe substitutes.
