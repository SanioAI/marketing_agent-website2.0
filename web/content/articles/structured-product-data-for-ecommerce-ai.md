Structured product data for AI is **not JSON for SEO**—it is **normalized, channel-aligned, constraint-safe** facts and edges agents and marketplaces can evaluate: the same density **Voomi** needed (**10–15+ attributes per product**, **1M+ SKUs**, **200M+ ASINs**), the same rigor **Profitero** enforces (**>95% precision/recall**, **80+ languages**), and the same browse alignment **JCPenney** needs on **Mirakl + SFCC**.

Teams ship schema.org while agents still cannot compare two SKUs safely—structure without **evaluation** is theater.
