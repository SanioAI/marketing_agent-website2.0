Compliance automation is **continuous classification + evidence** on SKUs: hazmat, restricted categories, multipack/bundle integrity—executed by **Compliance Agent** with measurable precision, not annual spreadsheet audits. Paladio does not replace legal sign-off; it **shrinks the SKU surface** legal must review by pre-structuring channel-safe facts (same class of work **Voomi** automated alongside **200M+ ASIN** matching).

Policy teams publish PDFs; marketplace teams get delisted SKUs; nobody can answer “which attribute change triggered this?” at **Profitero**-scale marketplace breadth or **Voomi**-scale SKU volume.
