## Tax incidents as downstream catalog defects

You might think taxability issues stem from faulty tax engines. But the real problem lies deeper—it's your catalog. When your **multipack errors** and **category drift** occur, no tax API will rescue you. These defects, often dismissed as minor, directly translate into tax incidents. You see, tax compliance starts with the data foundation. And if that base is unstable, tax errors are inevitable.

Your catalog is an intricate web of **SKU-level facts**—each one a potential point of failure. When the underlying data shifts, your tax calculations follow suit. Just like **Voomi Supply** showed us how multipack detection is critical for maintaining publish velocity across over 1 million SKUs, you need a similarly rigorous approach for taxability. Ignoring these catalog defects means ignoring the root cause of your tax troubles.

## Minimum SKU facts for defensible taxability

Most teams assume a tax solution is about plugging in Avalara or a similar service. But taxability is more than just flipping a switch. It's about having **defensible SKU facts** that stand up to scrutiny. Each SKU in your catalog needs to be aligned with specific attributes—category, pack size, shipping origin, exempt status, and B2B flags.

Without these, you're flying blind. Imagine trying to establish tax rates without knowing if a product is part of a multipack or if it ships from a tax-exempt location. That's a recipe for disaster. **Profitero's** example shows us the power of classification with over 95% precision and recall. You need that same level of accuracy for taxability to avoid running afoul of compliance.

## Multipack + category + ship-from graph

How do you ensure your catalog remains a bastion of tax compliance? By building a robust graph that seamlessly integrates **multipack**, category, and shipping data. **Voomi Supply's** automation for multipack detection ensures accurate pricing and tax calculations. Similarly, **JCPenney's** integration across Mirakl and SFCC illustrates the importance of taxonomy alignment for consistent tax paths.

Picture this: A multipack mismatch results in charging the wrong per-unit tax basis. That's a preventable error when your catalog's graph is correctly structured. This graph acts as the silent guardian of your tax strategy, ensuring every SKU fact is in its rightful place, ready to be translated into accurate tax calculations.

## TaxGraph + Compliance Agent handoff

The key to solving your tax woes is the **TaxGraph**—the intelligence layer that aligns with the **Compliance Agent**. The handoff between these two ensures that the catalog's integrity is maintained from data entry to tax calculation. As new products flow in, the TaxGraph validates and aligns them with compliance needs, catching errors before they manifest downstream.

Think of it like **Voomi's** automated detection—errors are caught before they go live, saving you from costly tax discrepancies. The **Compliance Agent** ensures that every SKU fact is matched with its tax counterpart, reducing the risk of incidents and maintaining a seamless tax process.

## Evaluation without inventing client tax %

You don't need to invent tax percentages to see the value. Look at operational KPIs to understand the impact. Consider the reduction in manual labeling hours, from 140 to just 20, as achieved by **Profitero**. That's the kind of efficiency you can expect when your tax process is aligned with a unified catalog strategy.

The compound effect is real. With each cycle, your catalog becomes more aligned, your tax processes more accurate. This isn't about making up numbers; it's about seeing tangible improvements in your operations. Every correction compounds over time, making tax compliance not just a goal but a sustainable reality.
