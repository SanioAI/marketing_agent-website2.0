## Tax Incidents as Downstream Catalog Defects

In the complex landscape of ecommerce, tax incidents are not mere errors—they are the inevitable byproducts of catalog defects. When SKU-level data is inconsistent or erroneous, it cascades into the taxability decisions made down the line. This is not simply a matter of flipping a switch with a tax engine like Avalara; it involves managing a dynamic, ever-changing catalog. The real issue lies in how catalog misalignments, such as multipack errors or category drift, contribute directly to incorrect tax assessments. A prime example is the **multipack mismatch** scenario, where errors in SKU facts lead to incorrect per-unit tax bases, causing significant compliance risk.

## Minimum SKU Facts for Defensible Taxability

To maintain a defensible position on product taxability, ecommerce and distribution companies must focus on detailed, SKU-level product facts. These facts include category assignments, packaging details, shipping origin constraints, exempt bundles, and B2B flags. Each of these elements plays a critical role in determining taxability. Without accurate, up-to-date information on these aspects, tax decisions are made on shaky ground. The **Voomi Supply** initiative highlights this need, demonstrating that **multipack detection** is a crucial prerequisite for any downstream pricing or tax surface that operates at scale. Without these foundational truths, tax teams are left to blame the proverbial “engine” for what are essentially data integrity issues.

## Multipack + Category + Ship-From Graph

The integration of **multipack**, category, and ship-from data forms the backbone of what we call the TaxGraph. This graph is not just a static repository of information; it's a dynamic layer of taxability intelligence that anticipates and reacts to catalog changes. Take, for instance, the **JCPenney** case, where a unified catalog layer for taxonomy alignment ensures that tax browse paths depend on consistent keys. By maintaining alignment across different channels, companies can avoid the pitfalls of category drift and ensure that their tax calculations remain accurate. This alignment becomes especially critical when managing **1M+ SKUs**, where the scale amplifies even minor misalignments into significant tax incidents.

## TaxGraph + Compliance Agent Handoff

The handoff between TaxGraph and the Compliance Agent is a critical touchpoint in the taxability process. TaxGraph serves as the taxability intelligence layer, providing the necessary inputs for the Compliance Agent to function effectively. This relationship is akin to the synergy seen in **Profitero**'s classification accuracy model, which boasts **>95% precision/recall**, dramatically reducing labeling hours from **140 to 20**. By leveraging TaxGraph, companies can ensure that their compliance checks are not only thorough but also efficient, effectively bridging the gap between data integrity and regulatory adherence.
