Taxability is not “turn on Avalara”—it is **SKU-level product facts** (category, pack, shipping origin constraints, exempt bundles, B2B flags) kept consistent as catalogs change. **TaxGraph** (per Paladio capability map in `research/paladio-case-context.md`) is the **taxability intelligence layer** that must stay aligned with **Compliance Agent** outputs (e.g., **multipack** truth from **Voomi**-class automation) and channel taxonomies (**JCPenney** / **Mirakl** alignment).

Tax teams blame “the engine” while catalog ships **multipack** errors and category drift that no tax API can infer—same class of defects **Voomi** eliminated for publish velocity at **1M+ SKUs**.
