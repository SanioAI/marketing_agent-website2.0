## Handoff to Channel Matching

The complexity of ecommerce operations often requires seamless handoffs between different systems and processes. When an ASIN family is mismatched, the Compliance Agent facilitates an efficient transition to channel matching. This involves aligning incorrect ASIN assignments with the correct marketplace categories, ensuring that listings remain compliant and visible. This process is crucial in preventing scenarios where merchants might override flagged issues without a thorough review, leading to delistings—a situation no ecommerce operator wants to face.
