## Why your catalog drifts after every supplier update

You might think your catalog problems stem from outdated tools or insufficiently detailed processes. But the real issue isn't your tooling—it's the relentless volume and complexity of product data. Every supplier update isn't just a tweak; it's a potential source of drift that can throw your entire operation off course. You assume a new upload is clean, but hidden inaccuracies are lurking. The result? Your catalog drifts, and the errors snowball.

**Treating catalog problems as a one-time project.** Teams run a cleanup. The data looks right for 3 months. New suppliers send files. Drift starts again.

**Ignoring channel-specific compliance.** You think a one-size-fits-all approach works. It doesn’t. Each marketplace has unique requirements. You miss Prop 65 analogs or restricted categories, and suddenly, listings are non-compliant.

**Over-relying on manual overrides.** An agent flags a multipack; you override without review. The product gets delisted. The alert alone isn't enough—you need evidence that backs up why that SKU was flagged.

## What “detect” means (blocking vs warning)

Detection isn't just about raising a flag. It's about **preventing errors before they become problems**. A simple alert doesn't cut it. You need a system that actively blocks problematic listings from going live, not just a warning that may get ignored in the rush to publish.

The **Voomi Supply** model shows us the power of proactive detection. With over **1M+ SKUs** and **200M+ ASINs**, they achieved an **~85% faster publish time** by automating hazmat and multipack detection. This isn’t just about catching errors; it's about ensuring they never see the light of day. 

When detection is done right, the backlog never forms. Errors are caught before they impact your sales or compliance standing. It’s a system that doesn’t just warn but blocks and prevents.

## Signals: multipack, hazmat, restricted, prop 65 analogs (channel-specific)

You can't treat all signals equally. Each one—whether it's a multipack, hazmat, or a restricted item—requires its own set of checks. Every channel has its rules, and failing to adhere to them can lead to product delisting or worse, legal ramifications.

Consider **Profitero**'s success across **1500+ marketplaces**. Their governance model, with **>95% precision and recall**, ensures that signals like hazmat and restricted categories are not just detected but handled correctly. They reduced labeling hours from **140 to ~20** per cycle by using weak supervision pipelines. This isn’t just efficiency; it’s precision.

Ignoring these signals or applying blanket rules means you're setting yourself up for failure. You need a pipeline that scores each SKU against channel-specific rules and captures evidence to support those decisions.

## Evidence bundle for legal/marketplace

When a SKU is flagged, the real question isn't just "what happened?" but "can you prove it?" Without concrete evidence, you're at the mercy of marketplace compliance teams, and your listings are in jeopardy. 

The solution lies in **evidence bundles**—comprehensive packets that document each decision made by the compliance agent. Whether it's hazmat features, pack structures, or restricted categories, these bundles provide the actionable evidence you need for legal queries or marketplace disputes.

This is where **JCPenney**'s approach shines. By integrating **Mirakl with SFCC**, they ensure that detection outputs are channel-safe, aligning with marketplace requirements. This isn’t just about identifying issues but providing the proof needed to defend your listings.

## Handoff to Channel Matching when ASIN family wrong

Even when your detection is spot on, the work doesn't stop. Sometimes, a flagged SKU needs to be matched correctly within its ASIN family, and that's where channel matching comes in. If your ASIN family is wrong, it’s not just a compliance issue; it’s a revenue killer.

The handoff to channel matching is crucial. When a SKU is flagged, it should be seamlessly transitioned to the next stage in your pipeline, where it can be reassessed and matched correctly. This isn’t about passing the buck; it’s about ensuring every SKU finds its rightful place.

**Voomi Supply** replaced rule-based and VA workflows with an automated system that handles these transitions smoothly. The compound effect of each cycle means every run makes your catalog more accurate, reducing errors and increasing trust with your marketplace partners.

Every time your system runs, it learns. Every cycle compounds the gains, making your catalog more resilient, more compliant, and more competitive. This is the future of catalog management, where precision and efficiency are not just goals but realities.
