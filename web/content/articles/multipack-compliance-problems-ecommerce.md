**Multipack** errors are **joint compliance + channel + tax** defects: wrong inner count breaks hazmat ship class, ASIN family match, and per-unit tax basis. **Voomi** explicitly automated **multipack detection** at **1M+ SKUs** as part of publish automation that cut time **~85%**—Paladio treats multipack as a **first-class agent signal**, not a merchandising footnote.

Listings go live as single when they are 2-pack; compliance and tax teams discover it from chargebacks, not from PIM validation.


## Proof Points

- **Voomi Supply:** **multipack detection** listed alongside **hazmat** and matching in automation that supported **~85% publish-time** reduction on **1M+ SKUs** / **200M+ ASINs**.  
- **Profitero:** structured label precision **>95% P/R**—multipack is another label family.  
- **JCPenney:** **Mirakl** variant trees must match supplier pack data.  
- **Failure scenario:** 1-pack ASIN selected for 2-pack SKU → suppression + wrong tax basis.
