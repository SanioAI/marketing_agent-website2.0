## Why your catalog drifts after every supplier update

Most assume hazmat catalog issues stem from a lack of clarity in regulations. But in reality, it's about **signals, not prose**. Hazmat classification isn't just a legal checkbox; it's a precise orchestration of signals — from UN/DOT codes to aerosol flags. Each SKU must align with these parameters to avoid marketplace suppressions. When supplier data sheets conflict with carrier manifests, you face an avalanche of errors. The problem isn't the absence of rules; it's the misalignment of structured classification with operational data.

## Channel-specific restricted lists vs carrier rules

You might believe a single hazmat classification suffices across platforms. But the real challenge lies in **channel-specific restricted lists clashing with carrier regulations**. Amazon's hazmat rules don't mirror Walmart's, and DTC channels introduce their own quirks. Misclassifications lead to suppressions or, worse, safety violations. Imagine listing a multipack SKU as a single unit without considering inner-pack geometry. This oversight not only misaligns shipping classes but compounds the error across **1500+ marketplaces**. Your catalog isn't just a data repository — it's a dynamic system that must adapt to each channel's rules.

## How Compliance Agent proposes class + evidence

Faced with these complexities, you need more than just manual checks. Enter the **Compliance Agent**, which doesn't just guess but proposes classifications with evidence. It operates like **Profitero**, focusing on **precision/recall** rather than subjective interpretations. Each classification comes with a confidence score, and when uncertainty arises, human-in-the-loop (HITL) validation steps in. This isn't about automation replacing expertise; it's about augmenting your team's capabilities with scalable, data-driven insights.

## Multipack + hazmat interaction (Voomi pattern)

You may overlook the significance of multipack configurations in hazmat classification, but this is where many catalog efforts falter. A single-unit hazmat SKU can shift classes when transformed into a multipack, a nuance often missed. **Voomi Supply** has shown that integrating hazmat compliance with **1M+ SKUs** requires an understanding of how pack geometry affects classification. By treating each configuration as a publish gate, you ensure that every hazmat SKU is accurately represented, reducing instances of marketplace suppression and shipment halts.

## Evaluation: P/R on holdout SKUs (Profitero-style)

The efficacy of any compliance system is best evaluated against a holdout set of SKUs. **Profitero** sets the gold standard with **>95% precision/recall** for high-risk labels, and your hazmat classifications should meet this benchmark. This isn't just about avoiding errors; it's about building a resilient system that learns and improves over time. By consistently evaluating performance against a holdout dataset, you ensure that your catalog's accuracy compounds over time, reducing risk and enhancing marketplace performance.

## Why your catalog needs a scanner + audit

The assumption that your current system is good enough is a dangerous one. Hazmat classifications are not static labels; they are dynamic, high-risk elements that require constant vigilance. With the right tools, like a scanner and audit system, you can continuously monitor and refine your catalog, ensuring that every SKU meets the necessary compliance standards without manual intervention. Over time, this proactive approach compounds, leading to a more reliable, accurate, and competitive catalog.
