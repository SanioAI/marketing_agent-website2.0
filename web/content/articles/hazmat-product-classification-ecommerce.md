Hazmat is a **structured classification + packaging** problem: UN/DOT-style signals, volume, aerosol/battery flags, and channel-specific restricted lists must align on the SKU—not “legal said yes in email.” **Compliance Agent** scores hazmat like **Profitero** scores product labels: with **precision/recall** targets and evidence, and like **Voomi** treats **hazmat compliance** as a **publish gate** at scale.

Marketplace suppressions spike when supplier sheets disagree with carrier manifests; teams cannot reproduce which attribute version triggered hazmat escalation across **1500+ marketplace** surfaces (**Profitero**-scale) or **1M+ SKU** feeds (**Voomi**-scale).
