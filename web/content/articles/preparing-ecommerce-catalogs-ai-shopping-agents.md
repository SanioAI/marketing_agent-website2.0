## Most Catalogs Don't Fail Because the Products Are Bad. They Fail Because the Product Data is Wrong.

You've been sold AI as a magic bullet for ecommerce, but the reality is more nuanced. The real issue isn't that AI doesn't work—it's that your catalog isn't ready for AI shopping agents. While you're focused on integrating the latest tech, what truly needs attention is the phased readiness of your catalog. It's not about aesthetics; it's about decision-ready data. **AI readiness means continuous data preparation**, not a one-time beautification task. Paladio positions itself as a **[catalog agent](/catalog-agents) layer** that ensures this readiness through sequenced stages. Let's explore how you can prepare your ecommerce catalog for AI shopping agents.

## Why Your Catalog Drifts After Every Supplier Update

The first step to AI readiness is identity and deduplication at scale. Most teams treat catalog problems as a one-time project. You might run a cleanup, and the data looks right—for now. But new suppliers send files, and drift starts again. The real problem isn't the tooling; it's the volume. You need a system that handles scale continuously. **Voomi Supply** is an excellent case in point, with a sequenced roadmap that unlocked an **~85% publish-time reduction** on **1M+ SKUs** by focusing on identity and matching before scaling new categories. Imagine your catalog never forming a backlog because each update aligns with your identity criteria, not just your visual branding.

## Why Your [Product Intelligence](/product-intelligence) Fails to Convert

Next, you need normalized product intelligence. You might assume attributes, taxonomy, and brand data are secondary to visuals, but the opposite is true. Without this intelligence, your AI agents can't make decisions. They might list a product in the wrong category or miss critical attributes, costing you the sale. To prevent this, focus on normalization. **Profitero** has shown that scaling requires more than syndication milestones; it demands classifier evaluation with **>95% precision/recall**. When your attributes are correct and your taxonomy matches across channels, the suppression that used to happen, simply doesn't.

## Compatibility Issues Spike Your Return Rates

Skipping the compatibility graph phase can be a hidden disaster. You might think it's unnecessary, but then your agent answers "fits my unit" queries with gaps. Returns spike. The truth is, compatibility intelligence is often overlooked. Your AI must understand product relationships—what substitutes for what and what fits with what. Without this, your system is blind to compatibility, leading to mismatched sales. If you want to avoid the pitfall of compatibility gaps, integrate graph intelligence early. Remember, every decision point is a chance for AI to get smarter, and that intelligence compounds over time.

## The Hidden Cost of Channel Mismatch

You probably think your listings are ready once you've exported them from PIM, but channel matching is crucial. Without it, you're duplicating efforts, just like **JCPenney**'s **Mirakl + SFCC** programs, which had to fix taxonomy discrepancies across brands. The real problem here is not just about matching listings but ensuring they're ready for each channel's unique demands. Fail this, and your listings are suppressed, never seeing the light of day. The fix? Establish a shared agent layer above your PIM exports to lock taxonomy alignment early. The suppression never happens, and your listings go live seamlessly.

## Continuous Evaluation: The Key to Sustainable Success

Finally, the need for continuous evaluation with catalog agents can't be overstated. You might assume the initial setup is enough, but ongoing evaluation is what keeps your catalog decision-ready. The solution isn't a static setup; it's a dynamic scorecard that evolves with your catalog. With a system like Paladio, every run makes your catalog more accurate, compounding benefits over time. Imagine a scenario where every phase has a clear owner, each with exit criteria tied to agent-decidable outcomes. As the catalog improves with each cycle, the compound effect of a continuously improving system becomes your new reality.

Your AI journey doesn't stop at integration. It thrives on sequenced readiness work—identity, intelligence, compatibility, channel matching, and continuous evaluation. With Paladio, you're not just preparing a catalog; you're building a system that evolves and compounds its effectiveness over time.
